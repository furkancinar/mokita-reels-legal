Mokita Reels Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Mokita Reels is an internal web tool used by our brand to create short product videos and, after an explicit Publish action, send them to our own TikTok account through official TikTok APIs.

Data we use

  • TikTok open_id, access token, and basic account name obtained through Login Kit
  • Video files and captions that we create in the dashboard
  • Product images and text that we upload or import for video generation

How we use it

We use this data only to generate videos, show connection status, and publish content that the account owner has approved. We do not sell personal data. We share data with TikTok only as required to authenticate and publish.

Storage and retention

Tokens and media are stored on our server. We keep them while the integration is active. The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time by revoking access in TikTok settings and deleting the stored token from our app.
